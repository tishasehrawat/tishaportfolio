About Tisha

I’m Tisha, an IB student in Bengaluru on the path to medicine. I’m fascinated by what happens beneath the surface — the signals a cell leaves behind, the way a brain remembers, and the fear a patient carries into a hospital. So I ask questions. I research. I build. And I keep coming back to one idea: science should mean something to someone.
