About Me

I’m an IB Diploma student at 10X International School, Bengaluru, working toward a future as a physician-scientist. I’m fascinated by what happens beneath the surface — the signals a cell leaves behind, the way a brain remembers, and the fear a patient carries into a hospital.

My work spans cancer epigenetics, neuroscience, clinical shadowing and healthcare innovation. At its heart is a simple belief: people come before the research.

What drives me

Guiding Principles

Curiosity

A passing detail in biology class — how one misfolded protein can disrupt neural signalling — became a published national review of prion disease in India. I follow questions until the mechanism makes sense.

Care

Every project I have built began with a person struggling: patients afraid in waiting rooms, families caring for someone with dementia, households drinking water they had no way to test.

Rigour

Comparing machine-learning models across more than 1,000 patient records taught me to test ideas against evidence, rerun what doesn’t convince me, and report results honestly.
