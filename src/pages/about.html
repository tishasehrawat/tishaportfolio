About Me

I’m Tisha Sehrawat, an IB Diploma student at 10X International School in Bengaluru, working toward a future as a physician-scientist. I’m drawn to neuroscience and oncology, and to the moment where a scientific discovery becomes better care for a real person.

Most of my work starts the same way: with a detail I can’t let go of. A lesson on gene expression led to machine-learning research on 5hmC signatures in cell-free DNA. A class discussion on neural signalling led to a published national review of prion disease in India. Watching patients and caregivers wait anxiously during hospital shadowing led me to build Project CARE.

I care about who science is for. That is why I co-founded Shiksham to bring STEM mentorship to girls in underserved communities, and why I spent hours listening to caregivers and doctors before designing anything for VisionXcelerate.
