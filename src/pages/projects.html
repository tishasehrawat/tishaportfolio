Each of these projects began with a person struggling — a patient afraid of what comes next, a family caring for someone with dementia, a household unable to test its water. Building them taught me how ideas become tools, and how much depends on the people who use them.

Founder & developer · 2026 — ongoing

Project CARE

A calm companion for patients and families during waiting, diagnosis, surgery and the moments in between. It grew from what I saw while shadowing at Fortis Hospital and Narayana Health: how much fear people carry into consultations and waiting rooms.

  • Three stages of support: capture fear, clarify uncertainty, create comfort
  • Available in English, Hindi and Kannada
  • A pilot that supports wellbeing and communication; it does not replace medical advice

Founder & lead researcher · 2025 — ongoing

PlastiXcan

A portable, low-cost system that screens food and water samples for microplastics. A controlled-lighting imaging chamber, a Raspberry Pi and OpenCV identify fibres, fragments and beads by shape and texture, and report particle counts and sizes.

  • CREST Gold Award, British Science Association
  • Indian patent application IN202641095036 A1 published (2026)
  • Prototype built for under ₹4,000

How it works

Inside Project CARE

A three-stage framework for the most stressful moments of a hospital visit, designed to sit alongside clinical care, never in place of it.

Capture fear

Structured emotional check-ins help patients and caregivers name how they feel before and during a visit.

Clarify uncertainty

Plain-language explanations of hospital workflows, so people know what to expect and what to ask.

Create comfort

Accessible coping exercises and support for waiting rooms and other high-stress moments.
