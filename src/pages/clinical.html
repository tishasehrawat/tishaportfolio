Clinical Experience

Research teaches me to look closer; clinical experience reminds me why. Shadowing physicians and volunteering in a community clinic showed me medicine’s science and its human side, often in the same moment.

Hospital shadowing

Learning Beside the Bedside

Across neurology, cardiology and oncology, I observed consultations, ward rounds and procedures, and watched how care teams reason through difficult decisions together.

What stayed with me most happened between the procedures: the fear patients and caregivers carried while they waited. Those observations became the starting point for Project CARE.
