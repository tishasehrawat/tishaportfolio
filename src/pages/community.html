Shiksham

Co-founder · STEM mentorship for girls in underserved communities · 2023 — 2025

70+

Girls mentored in STEM

I wanted girls who didn’t grow up with the same resources I did to have access to the same opportunities. So I co-founded Shiksham, an initiative bringing science, mentorship and holistic learning to girls in rural and underserved communities.

I helped design the curriculum, led STEM workshops and mentoring sessions, and built a volunteer team so the programme could reach far more girls than I could teach alone. We also raised funds for books, stationery and learning resources, and ran tutoring and wellbeing support.
